Privacy

What Library stores, where it stores it, and what it sends over the network.

On this page

  1. What is stored
  2. Your files
  3. What is sent
  4. Touch ID or password lock
  5. iCloud Drive

What is stored

Library keeps your library and clipboard history on your Mac. Unpinned clipboard history stays in memory and is cleared when you quit, unless you turn on Keep Clipboard Across Restarts in Settings > Clipboard.

Clipboard history never leaves your Mac.

Your files

Your files are in ~/.library/.

Files in ~/.library
FileContents
bookmarks.jsonYour items, folders, shortcuts, and order
clipboard.jsonPinned clipboard items, encrypted with a key kept in your Keychain
clipboard-history.jsonUnpinned clipboard history, encrypted the same way. Only exists when Keep Clipboard Across Restarts is on (off by default).
snippets.jsonYour snippets, as plain text. They are not encrypted. Don't keep passwords in a snippet.
favicons/Cached site icons. Library downloads them again if you delete the folder.

If Library can't read bookmarks.json, it saves a copy as bookmarks-damaged.json and tells you when it launches. Updates don't change your data.

What is sent

The app connects to the internet only for these:

  • Favicons from the sites you bookmark.
  • Copied GIFs, only if you turn on Play Copied GIFs in Settings > Clipboard (off by default). Library downloads a GIF you copied from a web page so it can play.
  • Link checks, only if you turn on Check Links Periodically in Settings > General (off by default): once a day, Library requests each bookmark's address with a HEAD request, which reads no page content. A server that refuses HEAD gets one GET request instead, and Library reads at most the first 64 KB of it.
  • Update checks to https://api.github.com/repos/deansasek/library/releases/latest. This happens when you choose Check for Updates…, and every hour when automatic checks are on (the default). The request names only the app's version, in its User-Agent header, and the ETag from GitHub's last answer, so unchanged checks cost nothing. No account or other data is sent.

Touch ID or password lock

Turn on Require Touch ID or password to show pinned items in Settings > Clipboard (off by default). Pinned items stay hidden until you authenticate. The unlock lasts in memory until you choose Lock Pinned Items, the Mac sleeps, or Library quits. Search doesn't look inside locked items. If the Mac has no password and no Touch ID, Library won't turn the lock on and explains how to add one.

iCloud Drive

Turn on Keep Library in iCloud Drive in Settings > General (off by default). It moves bookmarks.json into iCloud Drive and links to it from ~/.library, so your library follows you to your other Macs. Clipboard history never leaves your Mac.

If two Macs change the library at once, the newer change wins for each item, and Library tells you when a change from another Mac replaced an edit you hadn't saved.
